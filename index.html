About us

Food from the grill, served the way we eat at home

Cipher Eats started with one idea: the dishes we cook for family on weekends deserve a proper dining room. Our kitchen centres on the charcoal grill, with suya-spiced chicken, peppered beef and fish cooked to order.

  • Cooked over charcoal

    Every grilled dish is cooked to order, never reheated.

  • Small, honest menu

    Fewer dishes, made well, with fresh produce from local markets.

  • Easy to book

    Send a reservation enquiry online and we'll confirm by email.
